The four things that decide if a bonus is good

Headline numbers are easy. These are the bits you actually need to read.

📌

Wagering requirement

How many times you have to bet through the bonus before it turns into withdrawable cash. 6× is fine, 40× is rough. Look at this first.

⏱️

The time window

Most bonuses give you 7 or 14 days. Don't claim a bonus the night before you go on holiday - the clock starts the moment it's credited.

📊

Minimum odds

Bets at very low odds (under 1.80 typically) don't count toward wagering. That stops people from clearing the bonus on near-certain favourites.

🚫

Excluded markets

System bets, each-way, and certain low-margin markets often don't contribute. Read the list before you start.

Wagering, with actual numbers

The only way to know if a bonus is worth it is to do the maths once. Here's how on the easiest one - the sports crypto bonus.

Example: 100 USDT bonus, 6× wagering, odds 1.80+, 14 days

Step 1. 100 USDT × 6 = 600 USDT in qualifying bets.

Step 2. Bet 20 USDT a slip and you'll need 30 slips in 14 days. That's just over 2 per day.

Step 3. The sportsbook keeps about 5% margin on football. So 600 USDT of turnover costs you, on average, about 30 USDT.

Step 4. If you finish wagering with the bonus still alive, you keep roughly 70 USDT extra on top of your original 100 USDT deposit. Sometimes you'll lose more than the average and the bonus dies. That's how variance works.

Bottom line: the welcome bonus is basically a discount on the time you'd have spent betting anyway. It's not a money printer, and anyone telling you it is wants to sell you something.

Quick checklist before you click "claim"

  • Make sure the offer's actually live for your country and account currency

  • Skim the excluded markets list (system bets and each-way often don't count)
