Bet types you should know before placing your first slip

Rabona supports the full range of standard sportsbook wagers. Knowing how each bet settles helps you read the bet slip, set realistic expectations and understand bonus wagering rules.

Single (1X2)
One selection on home win, draw or away win. The simplest entry point - your stake is multiplied by the odds if the pick is correct.
Double chance
Covers two of the three possible match results (1X, X2 or 12). Lower odds, higher hit rate.
Over / Under (totals)
Bet on whether the total of goals, points or sets is above or below a line set by the bookmaker. Common lines: 2.5 goals, 220.5 NBA points.
Both teams to score (BTTS)
Yes/No market on whether each side scores at least once during regulation time.
Handicap
The favourite starts with a virtual deficit ( - 1, - 1.5, - 2…) and must overcome it for the bet to win. Used to level lopsided matches.
Asian handicap
Eliminates the draw by splitting stakes across half-goal increments ( - 0.25, - 0.75). Refunds or half-wins are possible on quarter lines.
Accumulator (parlay)
Multiple selections combined into one slip. All legs must win. Odds multiply, but a single losing leg voids the entire ticket.
System bet
An accumulator that still pays out if some legs lose. Example: a 2/4 system on four selections - any two correct picks return a partial payout.
Player props
Wagers on individual performance: goals, assists, shots on target, cards, three-pointers, aces.
Live / in-play
Bets placed after kick-off. Odds shift second by second based on the live state of the match.
Cash out
Closes an open bet early at the current value before the event ends. Available on selected single and accumulator bets.
Bet builder
Combines multiple markets from the same match into a single boosted selection (e.g. home win + over 2.5 + Mané to score).

Account verification (KYC): what you need and why

Rabona, like every regulated sportsbook, runs identity checks to prevent fraud, underage betting and money laundering. Completing verification early avoids withdrawal delays.

🪪

Proof of identity

A clear photo of a valid passport, national ID card or driving licence. All four corners must be visible and the document must be in date.

🏠

Proof of address

A utility bill, bank statement or official letter dated within the last three months and showing your registered address.

🤳

Selfie verification

A live selfie holding your ID may be requested. Use natural light and avoid filters or glare across the document.

💳

Proof of payment method

For cards, a photo with the middle digits and CVV hidden. For e-wallets and crypto, a screenshot of the wallet address or account email.

Verification usually completes within 24 - 48 hours. Once approved, future withdrawals process without re-checks unless you change personal details or hit higher limits.

Rabona Kuwait FAQ

Fifteen questions covering the most common topics users research before and after opening an account: access, payments, bonuses, the mobile app and safer betting.

Is Rabona legal to use from Kuwait?

Rabona is licensed and operated outside Kuwait under the jurisdiction shown in its terms. Local Kuwaiti law does not license online gambling operators. Users are responsible for checking the rules that apply to them personally and for using the service only if they are aged 18 or older.

What currencies can I use to bet on Rabona?

Accounts typically operate in USD, EUR or selected regional currencies. Crypto wallets settle in BTC, ETH or USDT and convert to the account currency at the rate shown in the cashier.

How long does the Rabona welcome bonus take to clear?

The standard wagering requirement is to roll over the bonus amount multiple times on qualifying odds within a fixed window - usually 5 to 14 days. Always read the live bonus terms before opting in, since the multiplier and time limit can change between campaigns.

Can I have more than one Rabona account?

No. Sportsbook terms allow one account per person, per address, per shared device and per payment method. Duplicate accounts can be closed and balances may be voided.

What happens if my deposit does not arrive?

Wait 10 minutes for instant methods or up to 24 hours for crypto and bank wires. If the funds are still missing, contact live chat with the date, time, amount and transaction ID. Cards and e-wallets are usually resolved within the same session.

Why was my withdrawal flagged for review?

Common reasons: KYC is not yet complete, you tried to withdraw before clearing bonus wagering, or the withdrawal method differs from the deposit method. Each of these has a quick fix - finish verification, complete the wagering or switch to a matching method.

Is the Rabona app safer than the mobile site?

Both use the same servers and the same security layer. The app adds biometric login (fingerprint, Face ID) on supported phones and push notifications for promotions and bet settlement.

Can I bet live during a match?

Yes. Live betting opens once the event has started. Markets pause briefly around goals, cards and other key moments while odds reset. Cash-out is available on selected single and accumulator bets.

How is cash-out value calculated?

The cash-out figure reflects the current probability of your bet winning. As the live odds move against you, the offer drops. As they move in your favour, the offer rises. You can accept it at any time or let the bet run to settlement.

Does Rabona offer live streaming?

Live streams are available for selected football, tennis and basketball events when you have a funded account or an open bet on the match. The list depends on broadcasting rights in your region.

Can I bet on accumulators with live and pre-match selections together?

Yes. The bet slip accepts pre-match and in-play picks in the same accumulator. The slip will lock briefly if any live odds change while you are confirming.

What is the maximum bet allowed?

Maximum stake and maximum payout limits depend on the sport, the league and the market. They are shown in the bet slip before you confirm. Limits may be lower while a bonus balance is active.

How do I set a deposit limit?

Inside your account, open Settings → Responsible Gaming. You can configure daily, weekly or monthly deposit caps. Increases take effect only after a 7-day cooling period, while reductions are instant.

How do I close my account?

Open Settings → Account → Close Account, or request closure through live chat. Self-exclusion options range from 24-hour time-outs to permanent exclusion. Any cleared balance is returned to your verified payment method.

Where can I get help if betting stops being fun?

Use the deposit limit, reality check and self-exclusion tools first. For independent support, organisations such as GamCare (gamcare.org.uk), Gambling Therapy (gamblingtherapy.org) and BeGambleAware (begambleaware.org) provide free confidential help in multiple languages.
